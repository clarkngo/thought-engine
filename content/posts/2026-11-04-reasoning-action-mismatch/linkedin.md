# LinkedIn copy: The Plan Is Not the Action

## Post

13.2%.

That's how often UC Berkeley's MAST study of multi-agent failures flagged "reasoning-action mismatch."

The agent reasons its way to one plan.

Then does something else.

It was the second most frequent of all 14 failure modes they found.

An agent's explanation is not a contract. Verify the action, not the reasoning.

Do you check what your agents do, or what they say they'll do?
