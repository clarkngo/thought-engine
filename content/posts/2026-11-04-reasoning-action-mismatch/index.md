13.2%.

That's how often [UC Berkeley's MAST study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657) annotated *reasoning-action mismatch*: an agent reasons its way to one plan, then does something else.

It was the second most frequent of all 14 failure modes.

An agent's explanation is not a contract. Verify the action, not the reasoning.

Do you check what your agents do, or what they say they'll do?
