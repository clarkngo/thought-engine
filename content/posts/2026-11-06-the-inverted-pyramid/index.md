39.8% Creating. 1.8% Remembering.

That's how [Anthropic classified Claude's responses](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-education-report-how-university-students-use-claude) across about 575,000 student conversations, using Bloom's taxonomy.

Students aren't using AI to memorize. They're handing it the top of the pyramid: the creating and analyzing we told ourselves only humans would do.

If the AI is doing the creating, what should students' creating look like now?
