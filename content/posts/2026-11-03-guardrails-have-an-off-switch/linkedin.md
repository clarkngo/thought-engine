In a large intro programming course, half of 885 students clicked "See Solution" at least once.

That button switched off their AI tutor's guardrails and gave them the answer.

Who clicked most?

Lower-performing students. Close to the deadline.

Guardrails don't fail in the design document. They fail the night before it's due.
