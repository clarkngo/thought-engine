50%.

In [a large intro programming course](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11146), half of 885 students clicked "See Solution" at least once, switching off their AI tutor's guardrails to get the answer.

Who clicked most? Lower-performing students. Close to the deadline.

Guardrails don't fail in the design document. They fail the night before it's due.

Are you teaching students to govern their own AI use, or just configuring the tool?
