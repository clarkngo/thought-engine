# LinkedIn copy: Confidence Is the Variable

## Post

The more people trusted the AI, the less they thought critically.

The more they trusted themselves, the more they did.

That's from a Microsoft Research and Carnegie Mellon survey of 319 knowledge workers and 936 real examples of AI use at work.

So "AI literacy" that only builds trust in the tool may be backfiring.

The goal is people who trust their own judgment enough to check the machine.

How do you build a student's confidence without building their trust in the AI?
