# LinkedIn copy: What Makes a Simulator Train

## Post

AI can now play the patient, the client, the customer, and the opposing counsel.

That doesn't make a simulator a training program.

This week's evidence points to four moves. Swipe through 👉

1. Feedback, not just reps. Practice alone made novice counselors less empathetic. Feedback made them better.

2. Rehearse the hard moment. Rehearsing a conflict with an AI cut escalation in a real one by 67%.

3. Then a check pilot. Pilots fly real flights with a check pilot after the simulator. The rules don't let the simulator replace it.

4. Measure the skill, not the hours. Hours in a simulator are not a learning outcome.

Who plays the coach? Where I landed this week: both.

The AI watches every rep and flags what needs a person. A person coaches the flags and signs off.

Which of the four is your team or program skipping?
