# LinkedIn copy: 92% Have an AI Strategy. 13% Measure the Return.

## Post

92% have an AI strategy.

13% measure whether it pays off.

That's from a survey of 1,960 higher ed staff by AIR, with EDUCAUSE, NACUBO, and CUPA-HR.

The use is already here. 94% used AI for work in the past six months. Only 54% knew of a policy covering it.

A strategy you don't measure is a hope.

(Caveat: staff perceptions. Some may not know whether ROI is tracked.)

What's the one number your institution should track to know if its AI strategy is working?
