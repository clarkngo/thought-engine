92% have an AI strategy. 13% measure whether it pays off.

That's from [a survey of 1,960 higher ed staff](http://www.airweb.org/article/2026/01/14/ai-at-work-in-higher-education) by AIR with EDUCAUSE, NACUBO, and CUPA-HR (fall 2025). 92% said their institution has a work-related AI strategy. 13% said it measures the return on those AI tools.

The use is already there: 94% used AI tools for work in the past six months, but only 54% knew of a policy or guideline covering that use.

A strategy you don't measure is a hope.

Caveat: these are individual staff perceptions, not institutional records. Some respondents may not know whether ROI is being measured.
