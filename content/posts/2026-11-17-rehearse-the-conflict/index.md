Forty people trained for a conflict. One group studied a lecture on conflict strategy. The other rehearsed with an AI that played the other side.

Then each faced a real conflict, with no AI.

The rehearsal group used 67% fewer escalating tactics and twice as many cooperative ones.

That's [Rehearsal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12309), from a Stanford team (Shaikh, Chai, Gelfand, Yang & Bernstein, CHI 2024). The AI didn't just argue back. It let people explore "what if" paths and gave feedback on which strategy to use, and when. [Same lesson as Monday](/posts/2026-11-16-practice-without-feedback/): reps plus feedback.
