5%.

That's how many students with AI built into their courses say it has transformed how they learn, in the [Digital Education Council's 2026 global survey](https://www.digitaleducationcouncil.com).

28% say it enhanced their understanding. 42% say it was only somewhat helpful. 24% say it added no clear value; in the US and Canada, 42% say so.

Adding AI to a course isn't the same as redesigning the course around it.
