# LinkedIn copy: Only 5% Say It Transformed Learning

## Post

5%.

That's how many students with AI built into their courses say it transformed how they learn.

28% say it enhanced their understanding.
42% say it was only somewhat helpful.
24% say it added no clear value.

In the US and Canada, 42% say no clear value.

Adding AI to a course isn't the same as redesigning the course around it.
