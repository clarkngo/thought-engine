# Research notes: AI job simulators (week of Nov 16)

Theme: AI as a simulator for the work juniors used to learn on the job. Follows from Nov 9 (the missing rung).

## Strongest sources (verified)

**CARE: Louie, Shah, Hasan Orney, Pacheco, Brunskill & Yang, CHI '26** (arXiv 2505.02428, DOI 10.1145/3772318.3791821)
- 94 novice counselors randomized: practice with an LLM-simulated patient alone (P) vs practice + AI feedback (P+F).
- 75-minute online lab session (20-minute practice chat). Short-term effects only.
- P+F improved reflections and questions (d = 0.32–0.39, p < 0.05).
- Practice alone: no gain on those skills; **empathy worsened** (d = −0.52, p = 0.001). P+F beat P on empathy (d = 0.72, p = 0.001).
- Authors: "combining simulated practice with structured feedback is critical."
- Note: abstract says practice-alone "showed no improvements," but the body reports a significant change in suggestions. Don't say "nothing changed"; say "the skills that matter didn't improve."

**Rehearsal: Shaikh, Chai, Gelfand, Yang & Bernstein, CHI 2024** (DOI 10.1145/3613904.3642159)
- LLM simulates a conflict partner (Interest-Rights-Power theory).
- 40 participants, between-subjects, then a real conflict with a confederate.
- vs lecture-based control: escalating competitive strategies down 67% on average; cooperative strategies doubled.

**14 CFR 121.434 (operating experience), read from Cornell LII**
- After ground and flight training (including full flight simulator courses), pilots still must acquire operating experience "inflight during operations," under the supervision of a check pilot.
- Initial training, Group II (turbojet; Group definitions in 121.400, not re-read): 25 hours, at least 4 operating cycles.
- Consolidation: at least 100 hours of line flying within 120 days of the checkride.
- Appendix H (advanced simulation) explicitly excludes these 121.434 requirements from simulator credit.
- So: the simulator replaces practice reps, not supervised line experience. "Straight to the 737" is supervised, with a check pilot.

**Giglio et al., JAMA Surgery 2025** (doi 10.1001/jamasurg.2025.2564)
- 88 medical students, surgical simulation. Expert instruction informed by AI data vs AI tutoring alone.
- Only the human-expert + AI-data arm improved significantly in both performance and transfer to a more complex scenario.
- Counterpoint: a crossover RCT in npj (Nov 2025) from the same lab found AI tutoring outperformed expert sessions. Mixed; cite carefully.

## Supporting / weaker
- LLM simulated patients for history taking: several small single-center RCTs (n ≈ 40–84; e.g. ophthalmology, npj Digital Medicine 2025, +10.5 points). Positive but checklist/OSCE outcomes.
- Atelier (Three Crowns + Stanford LIFT Lab): AI cross-examination simulator in a law firm's training program. Bloomberg Law op-ed by its builders; promotional.
- D2L / Morning Consult: 74% of HR leaders lack programs to replace on-the-job learning lost to AI (vendor survey; check primary before using).
- Tutor CoPilot is live assistance, not a simulator. Don't use as simulator evidence.
